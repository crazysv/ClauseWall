# ClauseWall Motion System

## Purpose
Motion in ClauseWall should make the product feel:
- calm
- guided
- responsive
- trustworthy

Motion should never feel:
- flashy
- theatrical
- cinematic
- distracting
- “AI startup”

## Motion Principles

### 1. Motion explains
Use motion to help people understand:
- where to look
- what changed
- what happens next

### 2. Motion reassures
Use motion to make the product feel steady and responsive:
- soft fades
- gentle reveals
- subtle hover feedback
- smooth step transitions

### 3. Motion stays out of the way
Nothing should feel like a showpiece.
Users should notice the product feels polished, not “animated.”

## Allowed Motion

### Page entry
- short fade + slight upward movement
- duration: fast to medium
- should feel immediate, not delayed

### Section reveal
- light stagger for grouped content
- use sparingly
- best for homepage and guided sections

### Hover states
- slight border/tone shift
- very small lift or emphasis only if it feels natural
- no bouncy interactions

### Product preview motion
- quiet clause highlight reveal
- annotation fade-in
- single detail emphasis
- no fake scanning or complicated storytelling

### Step transitions
- use for Analyze and Results
- should help users understand progress
- movement should be directional and minimal

### Expand/collapse
- smooth height/opacity transitions
- used for advanced details, clauses, and deeper tools

## Avoid
- scroll-driven spectacle
- parallax scenes
- sticky cinematic sections
- pulsing/glowing ambient backgrounds
- exaggerated bounce
- flashy loading loops
- hacker/scanner beams
- motion on every component

## Homepage Motion
- hero content can reveal in layers
- product visual can have one or two quiet highlights
- section reveals should be soft and sparse
- CTA hover should feel premium but simple

## Analyze Page Motion
- tab or mode switch should feel clear and immediate
- upload area can respond to drag state
- progress and scanning states should feel informative, not dramatic

## Results Motion
- summary appears first
- details reveal progressively
- advanced tools should expand cleanly
- charts and emphasis should animate only enough to orient the user

## Workspace Motion
- slightly denser than the homepage
- still restrained
- emphasize responsiveness over drama

## Stitch Guidance
When prompting Stitch, ask for:
- subtle premium motion
- soft reveals
- restrained hover feedback
- minimal stagger
- quiet annotation motion

Do not ask for:
- cinematic motion
- dramatic transitions
- dynamic storytelling
- immersive effects

## Quick Rule
If the motion makes the page feel more impressive than understandable, it is too much.
