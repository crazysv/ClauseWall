[A calm, trustworthy homepage for a product that helps ordinary people understand contracts before they sign them. It should feel human, grounded, and reassuring. Think civic guidance, consumer safety, and modern financial clarity. It should not feel like AI software, legal intimidation, or a dark cyber tool.]

**CORE SHIFT:**
- Move away from dark forensic intensity.
- This should feel more like a trusted guide than a threat scanner.
- The design should feel familiar, warm, and credible to a normal person who is anxious or unsure.

**HARD CONSTRAINTS:**
- Do not make this look like an AI startup landing page.
- Do not use glowing gradients, sci-fi styling, hacker aesthetics, or dramatic dark dashboards.
- Do not make it feel like a law firm website either.
- Do not make the page overly premium-luxury, abstract, or conceptual.
- Do not use too many cards, pills, floating widgets, or fake product stats.

**DESIGN TARGET:**
The page should feel like a blend of:
- a trustworthy public-interest consumer product
- a modern financial guidance tool
- a clear editorial explainer page

It should feel:
- human
- calm
- clear
- approachable
- intelligent
- practical

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- Platform: Web, responsive, desktop-first
- Palette: warm off-white background (#f7f4ee), soft paper panels (#f0ebe2), deep ink text (#1f2933), muted navy (#355070), moss green (#5b7f63), rust amber (#b7791f), soft red (#b85c5c)
- Typography: editorial but friendly. Use a tasteful serif or humanist display face for the hero headline and a very readable sans-serif for body and UI. Avoid futuristic or overly polished startup fonts.
- Components: gentle structure, low-radius corners, subtle separators, minimal shadows, real spacing, believable interface framing

**COMPOSITION RULES:**
- Build around one clear promise: understand what a contract means before you agree to it.
- Use fewer, larger sections with strong hierarchy.
- Show believable product framing, not marketing noise.
- If you include a product preview, it should look like an understandable contract summary with highlights and plain-language notes.
- Prioritize readability and trust over visual novelty.

**PAGE STRUCTURE:**
1. **Header**
   - simple logo/wordmark
   - restrained navigation
   - one clear primary CTA

2. **Hero**
   - plain-English headline focused on helping users understand contracts
   - short subheading that lowers anxiety
   - primary CTA: analyze a contract
   - secondary CTA: see an example
   - visual: contract page with highlighted sections and friendly plain-language explanations in the margins

3. **Why This Helps**
   - 3 grounded benefits:
     - spot risky terms
     - know what to ask for
     - keep track of changes over time
   - present these as guidance, not feature marketing

4. **How It Works**
   - three simple steps
   - upload
   - understand
   - decide what to do next
   - make this feel almost instructional

5. **Example Result**
   - show a realistic result summary
   - plain-language explanation
   - 2 or 3 highlighted issues
   - 1 next-step recommendation

6. **Trust Close**
   - short line about helping people make clearer decisions
   - grounded CTA

**CONTENT TONE:**
- plain English
- respectful
- reassuring
- practical
- non-hype

Avoid phrases like:
- AI-powered
- next-generation
- revolutionize
- legal intelligence platform
- transform workflows

Prefer tone like:
- know what you’re agreeing to
- understand the important parts
- get clear next steps
- make a more informed decision

**MOTION:**
- very light only
- gentle fades
- small reveal transitions
- subtle hover feedback
- no dramatic movement

**EXTRA INSTRUCTION:**
This should feel like a real, carefully designed product for people who need clarity under stress. It should feel more human than technical.
