[Layered contract results page that explains meaning before detail. Calm forensic analysis with plain-language guidance at the top and advanced tools hidden beneath structured sections.]

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- Platform: Web, responsive
- Palette: Deep Charcoal (#0b0d10), Panel Surface (#111418), Elevated Surface (#161a1f), Trust Cyan (#5ab2ff), Safe Emerald (#36c78b), Caution Amber (#e2a93b), Danger Red (#e05b5b)
- Styles: Precision borders, compact telemetry labels, readable body text, restrained accent use, structured density

**PAGE STRUCTURE:**
1. **Summary Header:** Overall assessment, confidence, and a plain-language explanation of what the contract means for the user.
2. **Top Risks:** Three to five clearly framed issues with concise explanations.
3. **Recommended Next Actions:** Cards or buttons leading to actions like negotiate, complain, compare, or monitor.
4. **Clause Details Section:** Expandable detail area for users who want deeper clause-by-clause review.
5. **Advanced Analysis Drawer or Secondary Area:** Space for proof systems, graphs, clause trees, or specialist tools, clearly separated from the main summary.
6. **Persistent Context Rail:** Secondary metadata, document info, progress, and saved status.

**SPECIAL NOTES:**
- Prioritize plain language over legal jargon.
- Hide advanced tools behind secondary affordances.
- Avoid dumping every feature into the top viewport.
