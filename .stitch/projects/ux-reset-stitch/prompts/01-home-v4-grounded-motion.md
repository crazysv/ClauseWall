[A grounded, trustworthy homepage for a contract-safety product used by ordinary people in India. It should feel like a real product from a serious team, not an AI landing page, not a startup template, and not an experimental concept piece. Add subtle premium motion, but only where it improves clarity and polish.]

**HARD CONSTRAINTS:**
- Do not make this look like an AI-generated website.
- Do not use glowing gradients, glassmorphism, abstract blobs, futuristic dashboards, floating marketing cards, fake analytics widgets, or decorative AI imagery.
- Do not use generic “three-card startup” composition unless it feels genuinely product-led and restrained.
- Do not make the page cinematic, theatrical, hacker-ish, or overdesigned.
- Do not design for investors. Design for a confused person trying to understand a contract.

**DESIGN TARGET:**
The page should feel like a cross between:
- a strong consumer financial safety product
- a public-interest technology product
- an editorially designed software landing page

It should feel:
- calm
- honest
- premium
- trustworthy
- self-explanatory

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- Platform: Web, responsive, desktop-first
- Palette: Charcoal background (#0e1116), panel surface (#151920), divider gray (#2a3139), off-white text (#ece7df), trust blue (#5b95d6), emerald (#3ea576), amber (#c89a3c), muted red (#cf6a6a)
- Typography: refined and human, not default AI-startup typography. Use a tasteful editorial display or serif for the main headline only, with a clean sans-serif for body and UI. Avoid tech-demo fonts.
- Components: low-radius or squared panels, 1px borders, subtle texture or tonal depth, no thick drop shadows

**COMPOSITION RULES:**
- Build around one clear narrative: understand a contract before you sign it.
- Use fewer sections, but make each section more believable.
- Prioritize realistic product framing over visual flair.
- If showing a product preview, it should look like a believable contract analysis interface, not a fake dashboard.

**PAGE STRUCTURE:**
1. **Header**
   - restrained top navigation
   - logo/wordmark on the left
   - one clear primary action on the right

2. **Hero**
   - one clear plain-English headline
   - one short subheading
   - one primary CTA and one secondary CTA
   - one grounded visual: a contract page with highlighted clauses, annotations, and plain-language notes
   - no giant abstract illustration

3. **Why People Use It**
   - present 3 very clear use cases in plain language:
     - understand what’s risky
     - know what to negotiate
     - keep track of changes later
   - this should read like help, not feature marketing

4. **How It Works**
   - 3 steps maximum
   - upload
   - understand
   - act
   - visually simple, almost instructional

5. **Real Output Preview**
   - one well-composed example of the analysis output
   - maybe a summary panel, a key-risk panel, and a next-step panel
   - keep it believable and calm

6. **Close**
   - short trust-building statement
   - final CTA

**CONTENT TONE:**
- plain language
- respectful
- helpful
- not hypey
- not legal-jargon-heavy

Bad phrases to avoid:
- AI-powered future
- revolutionary
- transform legal workflows
- next-generation intelligence
- unlock your legal superpowers

Good tone direction:
- clear before clever
- helpful before impressive
- real before flashy

**MOTION REFINEMENT:**
- Add subtle premium motion only where it improves clarity.
- Use soft entrance reveals, minimal stagger, understated hover feedback, and restrained highlight/annotation motion in the product preview.
- The hero can have a gentle layered reveal, but it should feel immediate and calm.
- Buttons and cards should have refined hover states, not bouncy or flashy interactions.
- If the page uses scroll-based reveal, it should be light and functional, not theatrical.
- Motion should support trust and comprehension, not steal attention.

**MOTION AVOIDANCES:**
- no dramatic scroll storytelling
- no parallax spectacle
- no sticky cinematic scenes
- no scanning beams or hacker-monitor aesthetics
- no constant pulsing or decorative ambient animation

**EXTRA INSTRUCTION:**
Make the page look like it was intentionally designed by a strong product designer with restraint, not assembled from common AI landing-page tropes.
