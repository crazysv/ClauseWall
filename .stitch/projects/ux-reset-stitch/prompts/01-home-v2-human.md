[A calm, credible contract-safety homepage for everyday people in India. It should feel like a serious consumer product, not an AI demo, not a law-firm cliche, and not a flashy startup landing page. The design should feel intentional, editorial, and product-led.]

**IMPORTANT AVOIDANCES:**
- Do not make this look like a generic AI website.
- Avoid glowing sci-fi interfaces, fake dashboards, floating glass cards, generic gradients, robot visuals, chat bubbles, abstract AI imagery, or “future of legal tech” vibes.
- Avoid overusing pills, badges, neon highlights, purple accents, and over-animated hero sections.
- Avoid making the page feel intimidating, theatrical, or too technical.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- Platform: Web, responsive, desktop-first
- Palette: Deep charcoal background (#0d1014), off-black panels (#13171c), soft steel separators (#2a3139), trust blue (#4d9de0), emerald (#3fb980), amber (#d6a441), muted red (#d96a6a), warm off-white text (#ece7df)
- Typography: Distinct, premium, human-feeling typography. Pair a refined serif or editorial display face for headlines with a clean readable sans for body text. Do not use default AI-startup typography.
- Styles: Minimal radius, precise spacing, strong editorial hierarchy, understated motion, subtle texture or tonal depth instead of obvious gradients

**PAGE GOAL:**
Help a worried or confused person immediately understand:
1. what ClauseWall does
2. whether it can help them
3. what they should do first

**PAGE STRUCTURE:**
1. **Header**
   - Simple, trustworthy navigation
   - Brand on the left
   - One clear primary CTA on the right
   - No crowded nav

2. **Hero**
   - One strong headline in plain language
   - Example direction: understand a contract before you sign it
   - Support text should reduce anxiety, not impress investors
   - Primary CTA: analyze a contract
   - Secondary CTA: see how it works
   - Use a grounded visual composition: document fragment, highlighted clauses, structured annotations, or editorial-style contract markup. No fake AI dashboard.

3. **Three Main Jobs**
   - Understand risk
   - Know what to do next
   - Track changes over time
   - Each block should feel like a product capability, not marketing fluff

4. **How It Works**
   - Three-step flow with very plain language:
     - upload or paste
     - see what matters
     - take action if needed
   - This section should be self-explanatory even to non-experts

5. **Example Output**
   - Show a believable preview of what the user gets
   - Use a calm, product-real layout
   - Prioritize readability and trust over spectacle

6. **Guided Pathways**
   - Three next-step cards:
     - Analyze a contract
     - Compare two versions
     - Monitor policy changes
   - These should feel like product pathways, not feature spam

7. **Trust Close**
   - End with a grounded CTA and a short trust statement
   - Tone should be: clear, helpful, serious

**CONTENT TONE:**
- Plain language
- Reassuring
- Smart but not jargon-heavy
- Avoid hype words like revolutionary, AI-powered future, game-changing, etc.

**MOTION:**
- Use only subtle reveal and hover transitions
- No dramatic scroll storytelling
- No cinematic animation sequences

**VISUAL REFERENCES TO AIM FOR:**
- premium editorial product page
- modern public-interest technology
- consumer financial safety tool
- serious, calm software for important decisions
