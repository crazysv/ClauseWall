[A distinctive, editorial, asymmetrical homepage for a contract-safety product that feels memorable and art-directed without becoming abstract, flashy, or AI-generated. It should have stronger visual character than a normal SaaS site, but still feel clear and usable.]

**VARIANT GOAL:**
Push the homepage toward a more distinctive editorial identity while preserving clarity and trust.

**KEEP FROM V6:**
- asymmetry
- annotated-document language
- non-template composition
- grounded product story

**PUSH FURTHER:**
- stronger typography
- more memorable composition
- slightly bolder visual rhythm
- sharper art direction

**HARD CONSTRAINTS:**
- no generic AI startup tropes
- no glowing tech effects
- no fake futuristic visuals
- no feature-spam grid
- no overcomplicated motion

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- Platform: Web, responsive, desktop-first
- Palette: paper beige (#f1ece2), faded oat (#e5dccd), near-black ink (#1b2028), storm blue (#4c6987), forest green (#55725d), ochre amber (#b27a35), muted brick (#ad645f)
- Typography: high-character editorial serif or display for headings, paired with a clean modern sans-serif for body/UI
- Components: varied block sizing, thin dividers, low-radius panels, subtle texture, more composition than component repetition

**PAGE STRUCTURE:**
1. **Header**
   - minimal, poised, well-spaced

2. **Hero**
   - bold but clear headline
   - tighter, more art-directed text block
   - contract preview or annotated document composition with stronger editorial layout
   - CTAs remain simple and readable

3. **Why It Matters**
   - not symmetrical cards
   - maybe staggered notes, callouts, or layered editorial blocks
   - should still read cleanly

4. **How It Works**
   - one of the most structured sections on the page
   - helpful and instructional
   - balances the bolder hero composition

5. **Result Preview**
   - composed as a visual essay on the product output
   - should feel memorable but usable

6. **Close**
   - concise, elegant CTA area

**TONE:**
- clear
- assured
- thoughtful
- less warm than Variant 7
- less utilitarian than Variant 8

**MOTION:**
- subtle editorial reveal
- refined hover transitions
- no spectacle

**EXTRA INSTRUCTION:**
This version should be the most visually distinctive of the three variants, but it must still feel like a real product homepage and not a design exercise.
