[A human, warm, editorial homepage for a product that helps ordinary people understand contracts before they sign them. This should feel emotionally reassuring, grounded, and approachable. It should not feel like AI software, a startup landing page, or a dark legal tool.]

**VARIANT GOAL:**
Push the homepage toward warmth, reassurance, and plain-language trust.

**KEEP FROM V6:**
- asymmetrical composition
- editorial structure
- annotated contract visual language
- minimal, restrained motion

**PUSH FURTHER:**
- warmer emotional tone
- softer consumer-trust feel
- more reassuring copy
- less sharp/technical framing

**HARD CONSTRAINTS:**
- no generic AI-site tropes
- no futuristic UI
- no heavy dashboard framing
- no same-sized card grid
- no harsh dark-mode intimidation

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- Platform: Web, responsive, desktop-first
- Palette: warm paper (#f6f2e9), soft parchment panels (#ece4d7), deep ink (#232a33), muted denim (#5c7391), moss green (#6c8a6a), clay amber (#bc8b47), softened red (#b96a68)
- Typography: warm editorial serif for the main headline and select emphasis moments, with a clear friendly sans-serif for body and UI
- Components: thin borders, low-radius edges, minimal shadows, generous whitespace, softer tonal transitions

**PAGE STRUCTURE:**
1. **Header**
   - simple and restrained
   - friendly but not playful
   - one clear CTA

2. **Hero**
   - plain-English promise
   - emotionally calming subtext
   - one strong CTA
   - one secondary “see how it works” CTA
   - annotated contract visual that feels understandable and human, not technical

3. **What This Helps With**
   - not cards
   - more like a composed row of short, supportive statements or note blocks
   - focus on clarity, confidence, and next steps

4. **How It Works**
   - simple, welcoming, instructional
   - should feel easy, not procedural

5. **Example Result**
   - a calm product preview
   - clear issue summary
   - plain-language next step

6. **Close**
   - reassuring CTA
   - trust-building final statement

**TONE:**
- gentle
- respectful
- clear
- practical
- steady

**MOTION:**
- very light fades and reveals
- minimal hover feedback
- no dramatic choreography

**EXTRA INSTRUCTION:**
This version should feel the most emotionally reassuring of the homepage variants. The user should feel looked after, not confronted.
