[A visually rich, world-built homepage for a contract-safety product that helps ordinary people understand contracts before they sign them. This should feel premium, immersive, layered, and unexpectedly beautiful, with a stronger sense of atmosphere, tactile materiality, and visual richness. It must still remain readable, trustworthy, and product-led.]

**NEW REFINEMENT GOAL:**
- Keep the strong expressive morphic direction.
- Push it further into a richer, more world-built experience.
- Make it feel more luxurious, dimensional, and surprising.
- Add more visual richness, more composition depth, and more atmosphere.
- It should feel like a high-end product universe, not just a homepage layout.

**IMPORTANT BOUNDARY:**
- Richer is good.
- Random is bad.
- Surprising is good.
- Confusing is bad.
- The product must still feel clear, trustworthy, and usable.

**HARD CONSTRAINTS:**
- Do not fall back into generic startup website structure.
- Do not use repetitive equal cards.
- Do not make the page feel like a fake AI tool or a crypto/fintech landing page.
- Do not become cluttered, noisy, or ornamental for no reason.
- Do not lose the central contract-first story.

**DESIGN TARGET:**
The page should feel like a blend of:
- luxury editorial digital design
- immersive product storytelling
- tactile interface sculpture
- art-directed consumer trust software
- a beautifully built information environment

It should feel:
- rich
- immersive
- premium
- tactile
- atmospheric
- distinctive
- still clear and trustworthy

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- Platform: Web, responsive, desktop-first
- Palette: deep midnight navy (#0a1020), ink blue (#121a2d), layered smoked glass white (#f4f0ea / controlled translucency), brushed silver (#c9d2db), electric steel blue (#6f95ff), teal glass (#69c8cb), moss emerald (#74b08b), sunlit amber (#d8ac57), dusty rose-red (#cf7b7b)
- Typography: high-character editorial display typography for hero statements, with a refined sans-serif for interface and body text
- Components: layered morphic surfaces, blurred glass notes, sculpted solid panels, tonal depth, reflections, subtle gradients within surfaces, varied density, overlapping planes

**MATERIAL / WORLD-BUILDING DIRECTION:**
- Give the interface a richer material language: frosted glass, smoky translucent note layers, paper fragments, soft reflections, metal-like dividers, subtle luminous edges
- The contract itself can feel like a primary object in the scene
- Annotations can feel pinned, layered, or hovering with tasteful depth
- Some surfaces can feel like interface panes, others more like editorial artifacts

**COMPOSITION RULES:**
- Use stronger asymmetry than before
- Let the hero feel like a built scene, not just a layout
- Create depth with overlap, stacking, and varied scale
- Allow sections to feel more spatial and sculptural
- Mix large statement zones with smaller precise detail zones
- Use more unusual section framing and negative space

**INTERACTION DIRECTION:**
- Add richer premium interactions
- Hover states can reveal depth, edge lighting, or subtle panel motion
- Contract annotations can softly animate or re-emphasize
- Product preview can feel inspectable and alive
- Some layered objects can have restrained parallax or depth movement
- Motion should feel luxurious and controlled, not noisy

**PAGE STRUCTURE:**
1. **Header**
   - premium layered navigation
   - translucent or floating treatment
   - strong CTA integrated into the visual system

2. **Hero**
   - dramatic but readable composition
   - not symmetrical
   - text should feel anchored within a larger visual environment
   - contract visual should feel like a central object with layered notes, highlights, and depth-rich product framing
   - CTA cluster should feel embedded in the scene, not dropped below it

3. **Capability Section**
   - not cards
   - use sculptural, offset, layered content zones
   - visually distinct blocks for:
     - understand what matters
     - know what to question
     - track what changes

4. **How It Works**
   - a more structured, grounded section that restores clarity after the richer hero
   - 3 simple steps
   - still premium, but cleaner and more instructional

5. **Product Result Snapshot**
   - rich, dimensional, believable UI composition
   - summary, highlighted clause, next-step guidance
   - should feel like a premium application surface within the same world

6. **Closing CTA**
   - cleaner and calmer than the hero
   - distilled trust and action

**CONTENT TONE:**
- plain language
- reassuring
- confident
- intelligent
- not hype-heavy

Avoid:
- AI-powered future
- legal intelligence platform
- next-generation engine
- transform workflows
- revolutionary product

Prefer:
- understand the important parts
- know what you’re agreeing to
- get clear next steps
- make better decisions before you sign

**MOTION:**
- richer, more luxurious motion than previous versions
- layered reveals
- subtle object depth movement
- premium hover interactions
- annotation highlight motion
- quiet parallax where appropriate
- still no spectacle or chaos

**EXTRA INSTRUCTION:**
This should feel more like a designed world than a page. Push the richness, atmosphere, and premium materiality further than before, while still making it obviously usable as a real product homepage.
