[A visually rich, expressive homepage for a contract-safety product that helps ordinary people understand contracts before they sign them. This should feel premium, memorable, and art-directed, with tasteful morphism and depth, while still staying clear, trustworthy, and usable. It should not feel like a generic AI startup or a boring SaaS template.]

**NEW DIRECTION:**
- Move away from minimal restraint and repeated simple sections.
- Embrace a more expressive visual system with layered surfaces, tasteful morphism, depth, and more dramatic composition.
- Keep the page understandable and grounded, but let it feel more premium, immersive, and designed.

**IMPORTANT BOUNDARY:**
- Fancy is allowed.
- Confusing is not allowed.
- The page should still guide a normal user toward one clear first action.

**HARD CONSTRAINTS:**
- Do not make this look like a generic AI product homepage.
- Do not use cliché futuristic neon-purple startup aesthetics.
- Do not fill the page with repeated same-sized cards.
- Do not make the page feel intimidating, hacker-ish, or cold.
- Do not make visual experimentation overpower readability.

**DESIGN TARGET:**
The page should feel like a blend of:
- premium digital editorial design
- modern product storytelling
- tactile interface design with morphism
- a high-end consumer trust product

It should feel:
- expressive
- premium
- slightly dramatic
- tactile
- memorable
- still user-friendly

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- Platform: Web, responsive, desktop-first
- Palette: deep navy-charcoal background (#0b1220), midnight blue (#121a2b), mist glass white (#f3f1ec / low-opacity layers), muted silver (#cfd6df), cool blue (#7aa7ff), teal-cyan (#63c7c6), soft emerald (#71b98a), warm amber (#e0b35a), muted coral-red (#d97878)
- Typography: elegant high-contrast editorial display for the hero headline, paired with a refined sans-serif for body and interface
- Components: layered translucent or semi-translucent surfaces, soft morphic depth, controlled blur, varied scale, sculpted panels, premium hover states

**MORPHISM DIRECTION:**
- Use tasteful glassmorphism / soft morphism selectively, not everywhere.
- Surfaces can have layered translucency, blur, reflection, or frosted treatment.
- Mix solid and translucent surfaces so the page still has structure.
- Use morphism as a premium texture, not as the whole design language.

**COMPOSITION RULES:**
- Use asymmetry and layered overlap.
- Let the hero feel immersive and visually magnetic.
- Use one or two striking visual anchors rather than many small modules.
- Build a page with varied rhythm: some large sculptural sections, some tighter structured sections.
- Product visuals can feel elevated and premium, not flat.

**PAGE STRUCTURE:**
1. **Header**
   - refined, premium, slightly transparent or layered navigation
   - one strong CTA

2. **Hero**
   - strong, emotionally resonant plain-English headline
   - one concise explanatory subheading
   - one primary CTA and one secondary CTA
   - immersive visual: layered contract preview, highlights, annotations, and depth-rich product framing
   - use tasteful morphic surfaces around the product preview

3. **Capability Section**
   - not repetitive cards
   - use a more sculptural or editorial arrangement to show:
     - understand what matters
     - know what to question
     - track what changes

4. **How It Works**
   - should become more structured and clear after the expressive hero
   - simple 3-step flow
   - use a premium visual treatment, but keep it instructional

5. **Product Preview / Result Snapshot**
   - show a believable product result
   - summary, highlighted clause, and recommended next step
   - premium layered UI, but still practical

6. **Closing CTA**
   - distilled trust statement
   - clear action

**CONTENT TONE:**
- plain language
- reassuring
- intelligent
- clear
- not hype-heavy

Avoid phrases like:
- AI-powered future
- next-generation intelligence
- transform legal workflows
- revolutionary platform

Prefer:
- understand the important parts
- know what you’re agreeing to
- get clear next steps
- make better decisions

**MOTION:**
- use more motion than the previous restrained versions, but keep it elegant
- layered entrance reveals
- soft parallax or depth movement is acceptable if subtle
- quiet morphic highlight motion is acceptable
- premium hover transitions and subtle section reveals
- avoid chaotic or theatrical over-animation

**EXTRA INSTRUCTION:**
This should feel like a bold new visual direction with stronger identity and richer presentation. It should be clearly more expressive than the previous concepts, while still staying readable, trustworthy, and product-led.
