# Stitch Review Checklist

Use this after every generated screen.

## Reject the design if:
- it looks like a generic AI startup website
- it uses too much glow, neon, purple, or fake futuristic styling
- it feels like a dashboard before it feels understandable
- it uses too many floating cards or pills
- it looks like marketing for investors instead of guidance for users
- it feels intimidating to a normal person
- the first action is not obvious

## Keep the design if:
- a first-time user can understand the page in 5 seconds
- the hierarchy is calm and obvious
- the copy sounds human and grounded
- the page feels like a real product, not a generated mockup
- the composition has restraint
- it feels trustworthy and slightly premium
- motion is present but easy to ignore

## Homepage-specific questions
- Does the hero explain what ClauseWall does in plain English?
- Does it show one clear first action?
- Does it reduce anxiety rather than increase it?
- Does the visual language feel product-real instead of AI-generated?
- Would a non-expert feel guided instead of judged?
