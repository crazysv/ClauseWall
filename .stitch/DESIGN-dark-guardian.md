# Design System: Dark Guardian
**Project ID:** 7990834098475031539

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
This design system is for ClauseWall, an AI contract analysis tool for Indian consumers. The visual language should feel like a shield — dark, protective, and technically advanced. Users are scanning legal contracts for hidden dangers, so the interface should feel like a command center that spots threats. Every element should feel precise, authoritative, and trustworthy.

- Default Mode: Dark mode
- Mood: Powerful, protective, cybersecurity-grade trust

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- **Background**: Deep charcoal black (#0a0a0f)
- **Surface**: Dark slate (#141420)
- **Cards**: Slightly elevated dark (#1a1a2e)
- **Primary accent**: Electric blue (#3b82f6) for major calls-to-action
- **Text primary**: White (#fafafa)
- **Text secondary**: Cool gray (#94a3b8)
- **Borders**: Subtle dark (#1e293b)

**Risk Indicators (4-Tier System):**
- **Success/Safe**: Emerald green (#10b981)
- **Warning**: Amber (#f59e0b)
- **Danger**: Crimson red (#ef4444)
- **Illegal/Critical**: Vivid purple (#a855f7)

## 3. Typography Rules
- **Font family**: Inter
- **Headings**: Bold, tight letter spacing for high impact and modern feel
- **Body**: Regular weight, optimized for comfortable, sustained reading of legal text

## 4. Component Stylings
- **Corner roundness**: Medium (8px for cards, 6px for buttons)
- **Interactive elements**: Subtle glow effects for high contrast and visual feedback
- **Cards & Containers**: Glass morphism effects and subtle gradient overlays
- **Important elements**: Neon-like accent glows to immediately draw attention to critical clause analysis
