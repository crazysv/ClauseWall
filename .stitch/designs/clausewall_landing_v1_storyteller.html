Clause 14.2: Arbitrary Lock-in

"...the Licensee shall be liable to pay full rent for the remaining period if vacated before 24 months, regardless of notice..."

WHY THIS IS DANGEROUS:

This is a 'one-sided penalty' and is often struck down by Indian Courts if there's no reciprocal penalty for the Landlord. See Model Tenancy Act Sec 13.
