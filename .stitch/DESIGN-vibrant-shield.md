# Design System: Vibrant Shield
**Project ID:** 7990834098475031539

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
This design system is for ClauseWall, an AI contract analysis tool for Indian consumers. The visual language should feel modern, approachable, and empowering — like a friendly expert who makes complex legal language simple. This is NOT a boring legal tool. It's a consumer product that happens to deal with law. Bold colors, rounded shapes, and clear visual hierarchy make contracts feel less intimidating. The interface should feel like using your favorite modern app, not visiting a government website.

- Default Mode: Light mode
- Mood: Modern SaaS, approachable, friendly, playful yet trustworthy
- Style: Colorful but not overwhelming, smooth micro-animations

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- **Background**: Soft off-white (#fafbfc)
- **Surface**: Cool white (#f1f5f9)
- **Cards**: White (#ffffff) with colored left borders
- **Primary accent**: Bold indigo (#4f46e5)
- **Secondary accent**: Teal (#0d9488)
- **Text primary**: Dark slate (#0f172a)
- **Text secondary**: Medium gray (#6b7280)
- **Borders**: Soft gray (#e5e7eb)

**Risk Indicators (4-Tier System):**
- **Success/Safe**: Bright green (#22c55e)
- **Warning**: Vivid yellow (#eab308)
- **Danger**: Hot red (#ef4444)
- **Illegal/Critical**: Bright purple (#a855f7)

## 3. Typography Rules
- **Font family**: Geist or Plus Jakarta Sans
- **Headings**: Bold, slightly rounded feel for an approachable vibe
- **Body**: Regular, friendly, and highly readable

## 4. Component Stylings
- **Corner roundness**: Large (12px for cards, 8px for buttons)
- **Edges**: Rounded, friendly, and approachable
- **Card Accents**: Colorful left-border accents on cards to easily indicate risk level and add visual hierarchy
- **Buttons**: Gradient buttons (indigo to purple) for primary actions
- **Badges**: Pill-shaped badges for risk levels
