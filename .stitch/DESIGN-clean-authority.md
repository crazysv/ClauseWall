# Design System: Clean Authority
**Project ID:** 7990834098475031539

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
This design system is for ClauseWall, an AI contract analysis tool for Indian consumers. The visual language should feel like walking into a trusted law firm — clean, authoritative, and premium. Users should feel that this tool carries the weight of legal expertise. Serif headings add gravitas while sans-serif body text ensures readability. Gold accents signal trust and verification. The layout is spacious and unhurried, because legal matters deserve careful attention. 

- Default Mode: Light mode
- Mood: Authoritative, trustworthy, premium law firm digital presence
- Style: Minimal decoration, generous whitespace, letting content breathe

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- **Background**: Pure white (#ffffff)
- **Surface**: Warm gray (#f8f9fa)
- **Cards**: White with subtle shadow
- **Primary accent**: Deep navy (#1e3a5f)
- **Secondary/Trust accent**: Legal gold (#c9a84c)
- **Text primary**: Near black (#1a1a2e)
- **Text secondary**: Slate gray (#64748b)
- **Borders**: Light gray (#e2e8f0)

**Risk Indicators (4-Tier System):**
- **Success/Safe**: Forest green (#16a34a)
- **Warning**: Dark amber (#d97706)
- **Danger**: Deep red (#dc2626)
- **Illegal/Critical**: Royal purple (#7c3aed)

## 3. Typography Rules
- **Headings**: DM Serif Display or Playfair Display (serif, authoritative)
- **Body**: Inter or DM Sans (clean, highly readable sans-serif)
- **Strategy**: Mixed typography creates legal document gravitas with modern readability and clarity.

## 4. Component Stylings
- **Corner roundness**: Small (4px for cards, 6px for buttons)
- **Edges**: Sharp and precise
- **Elevation**: Premium document feel with subtle 1-2px shadows for slight lift, rather than heavy drops
- **Accents**: Gold flourishes reserved specifically for trust badges, verified status, and key highlights
