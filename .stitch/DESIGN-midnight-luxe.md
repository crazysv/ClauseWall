# Design System: Midnight Luxe
**Project ID:** 7990834098475031539

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
This design system is for ClauseWall, an AI contract analysis tool for Indian consumers. The visual language should feel premium and exclusive — like a private wealth advisor for your contracts. Dark mode with warm gold accents creates sophistication. Glassmorphism adds depth without clutter. Every interaction feels smooth and intentional. The tool should make users feel they have access to something powerful and exclusive. Less is more — whitespace and restraint signal confidence.

- Default Mode: Dark mode
- Mood: Ultra-premium, luxury fintech, like Stripe or Mercury in dark mode
- Style: Subtle background gradients (dark purple to dark blue undertones), smooth, elegant transitions, refined, not flashy

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- **Background**: Rich black (#09090b)
- **Surface**: Deep zinc (#18181b)
- **Cards**: Elevated dark with glass effect (#1f1f23 at 80% opacity)
- **Primary accent**: Warm amber gold (#f59e0b) - creates a "premium tier" feeling
- **Secondary accent**: Soft violet (#8b5cf6)
- **Text primary**: Warm white (#fafaf9)
- **Text secondary**: Zinc gray (#a1a1aa)
- **Borders**: Subtle zinc (#27272a)

**Risk Indicators (4-Tier System):**
- **Success/Safe**: Mint green (#34d399)
- **Warning**: Warm orange (#fb923c)
- **Danger**: Rose red (#f43f5e)
- **Illegal/Critical**: Magenta purple (#c026d3)

## 3. Typography Rules
- **Font family**: Satoshi or General Sans
- **Headings**: Medium weight, generous letter spacing, uppercase for labels
- **Body**: Regular, warm and readable

## 4. Component Stylings
- **Corner roundness**: Medium-large (10px for cards, 8px for buttons)
- **Cards**: Glassmorphism with frosted effect (backdrop-blur)
- **Borders**: Subtle inner glow on card borders
